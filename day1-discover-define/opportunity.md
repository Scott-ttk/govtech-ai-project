# Opportunity: Check initial application for additional information or corrections required

**Scenario:** B · ICA passport applications

All quotes and metrics are synthetic and labelled as made up.

## Problem Framework

### 1. Goal

Applicants submit applications with complete and accurate information

### 2. Problem

Applicants do not know what a complete application looked like for their situation

### 3. Context

Parents renewing for a child were the most confused group

### 4. Constraints

Applications contain identity documents and sensitive personal data. For the prototype, only use synthetic data

### 5. Success

Pass if at least 8 out of 10 applications have complete information, in the right format

### 6. Explore

With the right guidance, will applicants be able to find and fill in the correct information?

## Evidence (synthetic)

### User quotes

- e.g. I don't know some of the fields is asking for (made up)
- e.g. Some of my uploads keep failing (made up)
- e.g. I don't have some of the information required (made up)

### Metrics

- e.g. applications with complete information (made up)
- e.g. applications completed within 15 minutes (made up)
- e.g. CSAT (made up)

## Hypothesis

Users will fill in additional information and make edits to incorrect fields with some prompts

- Risk type: Usability (build a clickable mockup first)
- Kill switch: Prompts are useful in helping applicants identify and correct gaps in information
- Kill-switch signal: More than 30% of applicants get stuck in the application process for more than 30 minute
- Success signal: Edits correct at least 50% of missing or incorrect information that would have otherwise occured (made up)
